## What shipped

### Pure ladder (`crates/aq-server/src/stall/{policy,evidence,ladder}.rs`)

- **`ExecutionPolicy::v1()`** (validated):
  - 15 min evidence window and 5 min grace.
  - 2 nudges with 5 min backoff; at most 3 deferrals.
  - 10 min notify grace and 5 min stop-confirm window.
  - Replacement ceiling of 1 per lineage.
  - 60 min known-long-check allowance.
  - Escalation to `owner`.
- **`ladder::step(episode, evidence, policy, now)`** is deterministic. Time is injected in ms. It returns the next episode and at most one intent. The rungs, in order:
  1. Observing
  2. Suspected (records evidence explanations)
  3. Nudging (bounded)
  4. Notified (once per episode)
  5. Stopping (typed stop, then wait for confirmation)
  6. Replacing (within the B4 ceiling)
  7. Replaced, or Quarantined
- **Explanations hold the ladder:** provider backoff, registered wait, known long check, human control. `ObservationsUnavailable` raises one `MonitoringIncident` and never stop/replace/quarantine.
- **S5 non-progress:** `evidence::is_progress` counts turn boundaries, assistant text, tool start/result and questions. It does not count usage/token growth, user/AQ-injected text, provider errors or gaps. Heartbeats, repaints and transcript mtime are never inputs.
- **Progress keeps ceilings:** progress opens a new evidence interval. Lineage replacement counts live in `stall_lineage_budgets` and are never reset.
- **Unconfirmed stop:** a stop that is not confirmed within `stop_confirm_ms` quarantines; it never replaces. Cancelled, held, hibernated or terminal-owned admission suspends the ladder with no action.

### Persistence and engine (migration `0081_stall_ladder.sql`, `stall/{store,engine}.rs`)

- **Tables:**
  - `stall_episodes`: CAS on `revision`.
  - `stall_actions`: stable UUIDv5 `action_id` per `(episode, kind, ordinal)`, `expected_revision`, intent, receipt and delivery.
  - `stall_lineage_budgets`
  - `stall_nudges`: per message, records `inbox_only`.
  - `stall_quarantines`: scoped to the attempt/lineage, never the worker.
- **New F4 kind:** `session.stalled`.
- **`engine::tick`:**
  1. Reconciles pending intents that have no receipt.
  2. Steps the ladder.
  3. Commits the intent at the expected revision.
  4. Runs the effect through `Ports`, then records the receipt.

  Ports are idempotent by action id, so a restart between intent and receipt re-runs the effect once, with the same id.

### Production ports (`stall/ports.rs`)

- **Evidence:** one SQL read over `observation_events`, node `last_seen_at`, task hold, B3 cancellations and stop state. Input state comes from terminal input receipts and the controller principal.
- **Nudge:** goes through the F3 mailbox (`system_send_in_tx`, sender `system/stall_ladder`). If input is Unknown (a receipt in queued/received/injecting/delivery_unknown) or a human controller holds the pane, the nudge is stored `inbox_only`. It is readable in the inbox and is never injected.
- **Poll-time filter:** `agent_access::messaging::node_poll` (via `injectable`) withholds every stall nudge that is inbox-only, plus every queued nudge while a human controls the pane or input is Unknown. This covers a nudge that was queued while eligible and then became unsafe.
- **Other effects:**
  - Notify: F4 `observe_stall` (once per episode).
  - Stop: B3 `cancellation::cancel`.
  - Replace: B4 `assess_task` + `release`.
  - Quarantine: a `stall_quarantines` row. B4 `assess_task` then refuses that lineage with `stall_quarantined`.

### Owner action and runtime

- **`session.nudge`** is in the G1a action union (`agent_access/messaging/session_action.rs`).
  - Payload: `{operation_id, project_id, expected_generation, body}`.
  - It uses the same safe-boundary nudge path, with an owner sender.
  - It is idempotent by operation id. Authorization and reconciliation mirror `session.message.send`.
  - The schema snapshot `protocol/dashboard-actions/v1/schema.json` was regenerated.
- **`stall::runtime::run`** is spawned in `lib.rs` when profiled execution is configured.
  - It sweeps profiled attempts every 15 s, in pages of 32, with a 30 s tick timeout.
  - It is aborted at shutdown.

## Limits and follow-ups

- **Unsourced admission inputs:** `held` and `hibernated` are always false in `DbPorts`, and H4 registered waits are not sourced. The ladder honours all three once a source exists.
- **Worker side:** aq-worker gets nudges only through `node_poll`, and the server-side filter is the guarantee. The worker needs no change.
- **Policy tuning:** the policy is the v1 constant; there is no per-project override yet.
