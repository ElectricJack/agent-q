What `wake_schedules_continuation_on_other_node_when_original_offline` does:
1. Registers the wait.
2. Takes a `suspend` directive.
3. Parks with `RetainedSuspended`, carrying Source and Checkpoint receipts bound to `canonical_digest(binding)`. The disposition is `suspended`.
4. Resolves the decision and runs the H4 reconciler, which leaves a `ready` resume intent.
5. Enrolls and registers a second node.
6. Marks the original node 10 minutes stale and the second node seen now.

It then asserts:
- Calling `schedule` twice admits once and then 0. Exactly one `agent_wait_wake_admissions` row exists.
- The winner is on the second node at dispatch generation 1.
- The attachment passes `Validate`.
- The successor has a fresh attempt and a fresh session, attempt generation is predecessor + 1, and the lineage is the same.
- The successor session is Remote on the second node.
- The continuation is unchanged.
- Both remaining budgets are below the lineage maxima, so they carried forward.
- `differences.node.changed` is `true`.
- The wake row is `admitted`.

What `wake_cancelled_task_does_not_resume` asserts:
- With a `profiled_cancellations` row for the predecessor, `schedule` admits 0 and cancels 1.
- No winner exists.
- The wake is `cancelled` and the H4 intent is `retired`.

## How each requirement is met

- **Durable state.** Migration `0083_agent_wait_wakes.sql` adds three tables:
  - `agent_wait_suspensions`: the directive, checkpoint, parking report and disposition. A CHECK ties `suspended` to a `retained_suspended` report.
  - `agent_wait_wakes`: one scheduler row per H4 resume intent.
  - `agent_wait_wake_admissions`: one row per dispatch generation, enforced by `UNIQUE(intent_id, dispatch_generation)`.
- **Suspension is neither success nor failure.**
  - `wakes::suspend` issues an idempotent `SuspensionIntent` bound to the caller's binding and the wait's continuation.
  - `wakes::accept_parking` checks the report's binding and continuation against the directive and records the disposition.
  - Neither one writes a result report or a settlement input.
- **Exactly one continuation.**
  - `schedule` locks each due wake with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`.
  - It then reads any existing winner for the current generation. A retry finds that winner and inserts nothing.
  - The UNIQUE constraint backs this up if two schedulers race.
  - `resume_pending` is never a running state. A wake runs only once an admission winner exists.
- **Serialized against cancel, complete and supersede.** Inside the same transaction, the scheduler locks `profiled_authority_hot` FOR UPDATE. A cancellation insert needs a key-share lock on that row, so the two serialize. The scheduler then checks:
  - a consumed intent → `acknowledged`;
  - a retired intent → `cancelled`;
  - `profiled_cancellations` or an attempt cancellation id → `cancelled`, and the intent is retired;
  - a confirmed completion, or another holding attempt for the task → `superseded`, and the intent is retired.
- **Fresh authority.**
  - The successor binding gets a new attempt id and a new session id.
  - `attempt_generation` is predecessor + 1, on the same lineage.
  - The `ContinuationAttachment` is validated before insert. An invalid one holds the wake with `attachment_invalid` instead of admitting it.
- **Any eligible node, no warm start.**
  - A Remote predecessor goes to the least-loaded live node: `revoked_at IS NULL` and `last_seen_at` within the offline window.
  - The predecessor's node and sandbox are never required.
  - With no live node, the wake stays `resume_pending` with reason `no_eligible_node` and is rechecked later.
- **Central (F1a) predecessors** get a Central successor with `node_id IS NULL`. A worker node is never selected for them.
- **Fair ordering.**
  - Due wakes are grouped per project and ordered by B4 `fairness::order` (project priority, then service history).
  - Projects are admitted round-robin, and each admission is recorded with `fairness::record`.
  - Within a project, manual overrides come first, then the oldest `wait_created_at_ms`.
- **Budgets carry forward.**
  - Retries: `MAX_ATTEMPTS − max(started launches, 1) − launch_failures`.
  - Elapsed: `MAX_LINEAGE_ELAPSED_MS − (now − lineage start)`.
  - Cost: `NotConfigured`.
  - If either budget is exhausted, the wake escalates with `budget_exhausted`.
- **Retention is required.** The scheduler loads the unexpired Source and Checkpoint receipts from the suspended report. If any is missing, the wake holds with `retention_missing` and escalates after 3 holds.
- **Bounded launch recovery.** `record_launch_failure` marks the admission `failed`, bumps `dispatch_generation` and returns the wake to `resume_pending`. On the 3rd failure the wake is `escalated`.
- **Manual resume** (`manual_resume`) sets `manual_override` and makes the wake due now. It does not reset `wait_created_at_ms`, so the wake's age is preserved.
- **Recorded differences.** Each admission records node from/to/changed, boot, session kind, `profile.changed` and dispatch generation.
- **Background loop.** `wakes::run` runs every 5 s alongside the H4 waits loop and stops on shutdown (`crates/aq-server/src/lib.rs`).

## Limits and follow-ups

- An admission is a durable `offered` winner. Delivering it to a worker launch path (native offer/grant composition, then marking the admission `acknowledged`) is not wired yet.
- The profile is not re-resolved through the selector. `differences.profile.changed` is always `false`.
- Retention receipts are trusted as reported. Nothing verifies them against the receipt owner yet.
- `suspend` and `accept_parking` are library functions. They have no HTTP routes yet.
- No tests cover `retention_missing`, `no_eligible_node`, launch-failure escalation, or manual-override ordering.
- CROSS-REPO FOLLOW-UP (aq-worker): the worker needs to honor `SuspensionIntent` (H5): checkpoint, stop, retain, then send a `ParkingReport`, and accept a `ContinuationAttachment` on a fresh launch.

## Proposed design-doc changes (not applied)

`docs/architecture/*` and `docs/specs/*` were not edited. Proposed changes:
- Document the wake states `resume_pending|admitted|acknowledged|escalated|cancelled|superseded` and the hold reasons `retention_missing`, `no_eligible_node`, `attachment_invalid` and `budget_exhausted`.
- State that dispatch-generation uniqueness, together with the read-back of an existing winner, is the "exactly one continuation" mechanism.

## Out of scope

Worker-side suspension (H5), launch delivery, cost budgets and profile re-selection.
